The original area rule: connect adjacent upper silhouette points and all supported lower branch tips, then count white pixels enclosed by the lines and bicycle contours. Enclosed gaps within one silhouette are included. Line pixels and detected bicycle pixels are excluded. Regions smaller than 10 px² are omitted from the display. Areas are image pixels squared, not square metres or verified parking spaces. Aim at one complete row with the nearest wheels towards the bottom.
